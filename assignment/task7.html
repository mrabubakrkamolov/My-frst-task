How to Make Perfect Ramen

What You Need

  • 1 ramen packet
  • 500ml water
  • 1 egg
  • Sliced green onions
  • 1 slice of cheese

Steps

  1. Boil the water in a pot.
  2. Add the noodles and cook for exactly 3 minutes.
  3. Add the seasoning packet and stir vigorously.
  4. Crack in the egg and add the cheese slice on top.
  5. Serve immediately and garnish with green onions.
